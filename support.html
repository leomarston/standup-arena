Getting started

Install Standup Arena on your Apple TV and on your iPhone: it is one free app for both. Put them on the same Wi-Fi. Open it on the Apple TV first, then on the iPhone, tap Continue and allow Camera and Local Network. They find each other without a code.

Where the iPhone goes

Stand it on its side (landscape) next to the TV, back camera facing the room, at about hip height. Your spot is two or three metres (7 to 10 feet) in front of it. The iPhone tells you what to fix: put me higher, tilt me down, step back, more light. When it says leave me here, it is placed; during play its screen stays dim to keep it cool.

Starting a game

Raise both hands and hold them up for about a second, or press Select on the Siri Remote. A second player joins on the Who's playing screen the same way. Every menu also works with the remote: Menu goes back, Play/Pause pauses a match.

Checking the camera

On the iPhone, Check the camera shows what it sees and what to fix, even without the TV. On the Apple TV, Settings > Camera check draws your room from above with the distance and four checks: distance, phone, light and space.

The iPhone can't find the Apple TV

Make sure both are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network) and that Standup Arena is open on the TV. On the iPhone, check Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network and switch Standup Arena on. The iPhone joins the TV you played on last; to pick another, open the app's Settings and tap Forget this TV.

It doesn't see me well

Put the light in front of you, not behind, and keep bright windows out of the picture. Step back until your whole body is in view, move chairs and pets out of your space, and wear clothes that stand out from your walls.

Playing together

Two players stand side by side in front of one iPhone and play each other at tennis and table tennis, each on their own half of the screen. Archery goes turn by turn, and up to four people can take turns at the bowling lane: step in when your name shows and raise both hands.

Match photos

With Match photos on (Apple TV Settings), the TV can show a slideshow of your best moments after a match. You can save them on the iPhone with Save to Photos, or delete them. Switch Match photos off and no photos are taken.

Your data

Your records and settings are kept on the Apple TV. Deleting the app deletes them. More in the privacy policy.
